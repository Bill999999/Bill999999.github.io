Fruit Box

Drag the mouse to exactly enclose some apples so the numbers in the apples total 10. You get 1 point for each apple.

如何開始玩？ / How do I start?

  1. 將滑鼠移到橘色遊戲區域內。 Move your mouse over the orange play area.
  2. 按住滑鼠左鍵並拖曳，圈住想要的蘋果。 Hold the left button and drag to draw a box around the apples you want.
  3. 放開滑鼠鍵，若總和為 10 分就能得分。 Release the button. If the numbers add up to 10 you score those apples.

想換一組蘋果？按一下「Reset Apples」即可重新開始。
